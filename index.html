✦ Questions

The things managing partners ask us first.

How quickly will we see new clients?

You will usually see movement inside the first two or three months, with the serious results building from month six. We are straight about this on the audit call: SEO is the slowest channel to start and the cheapest one you will ever own once it is moving, because the rankings keep producing clients without a cost per click.

What does this cost?

Most of our firms invest four figures a month, depending on the practice areas they want to grow and how competitive their market is. On the audit call we will tell you what we think your market actually needs, and if the honest answer is that SEO isn't right for you yet, we will say so.

Do you work with other firms near us?

No. We take one firm per market. If we already work with a firm competing for your clients, we will tell you on the first call and point you elsewhere.

Is the content compliant with legal advertising rules?

Yes. Every page, article and profile is written around the advertising rules lawyers work under in your jurisdiction, whether that is a bar association, a law society or a regulator like the SRA. It is one of the advantages of working only with law firms: we are not learning the rules on your account.

Do you also run ads, social media or web design?

No. We do SEO and build the pages the rankings need. Doing one thing for one industry is the reason the results look the way they do.
