✦ Questions

The things practice owners ask us first.

How quickly will we see new patients?

Enquiries usually start within the first two weeks of campaigns going live. We ask practices to judge the work properly at 90 days, when there is enough data to see cost per patient clearly and the accounts have been tuned.

What budget does this need?

Most of our practices spend between £1,500 and £10,000 a month on ads, depending on the treatments they want to grow and how competitive their area is. On the audit call we will tell you what we think your area actually needs, and if the honest answer is that ads aren't right for you yet, we will say so.

Do you work with other practices near us?

No. We take one practice per area. If we already work with a practice competing for your patients, we will tell you on the first call and point you elsewhere.

Are the ads compliant with GDC guidance?

Yes. Every ad, offer and landing page is written with GDC advertising guidance and ASA rules in mind. It is one of the advantages of working only in dentistry: we are not learning the rules on your account.

Do you also do SEO, social media or websites?

No. We run paid ads and build the landing pages the ads need. Doing one thing for one industry is the reason the results look the way they do.
